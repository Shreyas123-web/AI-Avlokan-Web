Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a registration fee?

Yes, there is a nominal base registration fee. Some flagship events might require a separate small pool fee.

Can I participate in multiple events?

Yes, as long as the event timings do not overlap. Check the schedule before registering.

Is accommodation provided for outstation participants?

Accommodation can be arranged on prior request at a subsidized cost. Please contact the logistics head.
